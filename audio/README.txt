🎵  BACKGROUND MUSIC — OPTIONAL
───────────────────────────────
You do NOT need to add a file here. The site already plays a music-box
arrangement of the traditional "Happy Birthday" melody (public domain),
synthesized live in the browser, with a waltz accompaniment that grows
more celebratory as the experience progresses.

That's deliberate: no audio file means a small repo and no music
licensing worries when you publish the site publicly on GitHub.

If you'd rather use your own recording, drop it here named exactly:

    birthday-celebration.mp3

It's picked up automatically (see `music.src` in
src/config/celebrationConfig.js) and replaces the synthesized melody.
Pick something soft and romantic that loops cleanly.
